# Project-Rapido

**Tableau | Excel | SQL**

## Overview

Rapido Business Performance Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project designed to analyse ride-hailing operations, revenue performance and driver efficiency across multiple cities.

The project focuses on transforming raw ride-booking data into actionable business insights by tracking key performance indicators (KPIs), identifying operational bottlenecks and understanding customer demand patterns.

The dashboard simulates a business performance monitoring system that helps management evaluate operational health and make data-driven decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall ride-booking and revenue performance.
- Analyse ride demand across cities, locations and time periods.
- Evaluate driver performance and utilisation.
- Identify cancellation trends and ride fulfilment bottlenecks.
- Track operational KPIs to support efficiency improvements.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Bookings | Total number of ride requests |
| Completed Rides | Successfully completed trips |
| Total Revenue | Revenue generated from completed rides |
| Average Ride Fare | Average revenue per completed ride |
| Ride Completion Rate | Percentage of bookings successfully completed |
| Cancellation Rate | Percentage of cancelled bookings |
| Driver Acceptance Rate | Percentage of ride requests accepted by drivers |
| Average Pickup Time | Average time taken for driver pickup |

### 3. Operational Efficiency
- Ride cancellation analysis.
- Average pickup time.
- Ride completion trends.

### 4. Business Insights
- Identification of high-demand locations.
- Analysis of peak-hour booking patterns.
- Detection of cancellation trends.
- Identification of underperforming operational segments.
- Data-driven recommendations for improving ride fulfilment and driver utilisation.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Source ride-booking data containing trip, revenue, driver and location attributes.
2. **Data Modelling:** Establish relationships between relevant datasets.
3. **KPI Development:** Calculate business and operational metrics.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Build interactive visualisations, filters and performance indicators.
5. **Business Analysis:** Interpret trends, identify bottlenecks and derive actionable recommendations.

## Expected Business Outcomes

The dashboard is designed to support:
- Better visibility into ride-hailing business performance.
- Identification of operational inefficiencies.
- Improved understanding of driver utilisation and demand patterns.
- Data-driven operational planning.
- Performance monitoring across multiple cities.

This is an independent business intelligence project based on ride-hailing operations. It uses analytical modelling and dashboard visualisation to explore business performance and operational efficiency.
